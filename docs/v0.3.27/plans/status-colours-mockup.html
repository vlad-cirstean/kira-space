P766 task status colours: 9 saturated inks, own family

Proposal from docs/v0.3.27/plans/P766.md §2 "Tokens: status block". Nine names (grey plus 8 hues), down from 16. Recipe oklch(L 0.21 h) clamped to sRGB at each hue's chroma peak; hex precomputed. Status chroma 0.143-0.210 vs task inks ≤ 0.131; pairwise status distance ≥ 0.111, ≥ 0.08 from every task ink. Grounds and task inks/bands are the shipping dark tokens and P764 Pure-B values.

1. Status inks next to the 10 task inks

Top: the 9 proposed status inks. Middle: the 10 Pure-B task inks with their rest band. Status inks sit higher in chroma and away from task hues. Bottom: each status beside its nearest task ink with OKLab distance (plan table).

2. Chip styles

Left, recommended: solid ink chip, bg-task-status-x text-chrome (#131315 text). Right, alternative: 18% tint of the ink over the ground with ink text, the current treatment's shape at stronger chroma. Lightness caveat: the tint chip reads as another band, and low-lightness inks (blue L 0.65, red 0.68, purple 0.68) lose contrast against their own tint, worst on task bands and row hover.
