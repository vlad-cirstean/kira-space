## 2. Design

### Principle

Hue lives in the ground, never in text or controls. Text stays fg/muted/subtle. Borders, buttons,
inputs, badges, status chips keep their neutral tokens. Selection and focus stay neutral grey (P710).
One full-strength colour cue stays per surface, because faint tints of neighbouring slots are near
identical (min pairwise sRGB distance between 8% tints: 2.0 of 441; e.g. slots 3 and 14).

### Tint formula

Opaque mix over a fixed ground, so `--row-ground`, `HOVER_OVERLAY` and stacked surfaces stay exact:

```css
--task-tint:       color-mix(in srgb, var(--task-color) var(--kira-task-tint), var(--kira-bg-chrome));
--task-tint-hover: color-mix(in srgb, var(--task-color) var(--kira-task-tint-hover), var(--kira-bg-chrome));
--task-edge:       color-mix(in srgb, var(--task-color) 40%, var(--kira-bg-chrome));
```

- Ground is always chrome: card ground, card head, title bar and panel head then share one value, so
  details and review read identical. Panel head moves from bg to chrome+tint (darker by 1 step).
- Percentages are theme tokens in `tokens.css` (`--kira-task-tint`, `--kira-task-tint-hover`), so a
  future light `:root` retunes them without touching `ade`.
- `--task-color` stays the per-element input (inline style, as today).

### Option A: faint wash (recommended)

Intensity: rest 8%, hover 14%.

- Card: whole card ground `--task-tint` (head and branch rows). Border 1px `--task-edge` (soft,
  replaces full-colour edge). Selected: 2px outline full `--task-color` (unchanged) and head ground
  neutral `select` (unchanged). Hover head/row: `--task-tint-hover`. Ripple: dashed `--task-edge`.
- Day band span rows: same as card head.
- Task details (task panel): head band (`DetailHeader` incl. forward bar, alerts) on `--task-tint`,
  3px full-colour line under it (kept). Tab row and tab bodies neutral.
- Review window: title bar on `--task-tint`, 3px line under it (kept). Gutter, tab strip, panes neutral.
- Files window: same band as review (one component).
- Shelf, needs, sessions rows: keep the 16x3 mark; no row tint (dense lists turn into stripes).

### Option B: tinted frame

Intensity: rest 12%, hover 14%; body wash 6% over bg.

- Card: ground 12%, no coloured border (`border-transparent`), 3px full-colour rail on the left edge.
  Selected: 2px outline full colour. Hover: 14% (weak step: 1.15 vs 1.18:1 to chrome).
- Task details: head band 12% + tab row 12%; panel body `color-mix(task 6%, bg)`; section cards
  inside keep `bg-elevated` neutral.
- Review window: title bar, window gutter (`windowBody`) and tab strip 12%; panes neutral `bg`. The
  window reads as a coloured frame around neutral content.
- Shelf rows: mark replaced by a 3px left rail, rows untinted.

Recommendation: A. It passes AA for every text token on every resting and hover tint, keeps the
surfaces the user reads (diff, notes, chain) neutral, and changes fewest primitives. B reads stronger
at a glance but its hover step is barely visible and the coloured gutter competes with diff colours
(red/green additions) in the review window.

| State | A | B |

### Contrast (WCAG 2.x, computed over all 20 slots, worst slot named)

Dark (shipping theme), tint over chrome `#131315`:

| Ground | fg | muted | subtle | tint vs chrome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| today chrome | 12.56 | 7.24 | 5.71 | 1.00 |
| today hover field | 10.06 | 5.80 | 4.58 | n/a |
| today selected select | 8.12 | 4.68 | 3.70 (fails, pre-existing) | n/a |
| 8% (A rest) | 11.08 (`#b4b04d`) | 6.39 | 5.04 | 1.09 |
| 12% (B rest) | 10.31 (`#90ba66`) | 5.95 | 4.69 | 1.15 |
| 14% (hover A,B) | 9.91 (`#b4b04d`) | 5.72 | 4.51 | 1.18 |
| 18% (rejected) | 9.09 | 5.24 | 4.14 (fails) | 1.25 |
| B body 6% over bg | 10.48 | 6.04 | 4.77 | n/a |

- Ceiling: 14% is the strongest tint keeping subtle text at 4.5:1. Hover must not exceed it.
- Selected head stays `select`: tinting it drops subtle to 2.9-3.1. The pre-existing 3.70 for
  subtle on `select` is out of scope (P710 surface); listed as owner question 6.
- Full-colour outline/rail/line vs chrome: 5.02-8.31 (non-text 3:1 met). 40% edge vs chrome: 1.64
  (decorative; identity carried by tint + selection outline; flagged as question 2).

Light (no light theme ships; mockup previews a zinc light ground chrome `#f4f4f5`, bg `#ffffff`,
fg `#18181b`, muted `#52525b`):

| Ground | fg | muted | subtle `#71717a` | subtle `#63636b` (proposed) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| chrome | 16.12 | 7.03 | 4.40 | 5.42 |
| 8% | 14.86 | 6.48 | 4.05 (fails) | 4.99 |

### Shared design: `AdeTaskBand` + `task-tint` utilities

One component, one token family, so task details and review cannot drift:

- `apps/kira-space/frontend/src/ade/v2/taskTint.css` (imported beside `tones.css`): Tailwind v4
  `@utility task-tint` (sets `--task-tint`, `--task-tint-hover`, `--task-edge` from `--task-color`
  and paints `background-color: var(--task-tint)`), `@utility task-tint-vars` (variables only, for the
  card where rows pick the ground). Utilities, not a scoped `<style>`.
- `packages/theme/src/tokens.css`: `--kira-task-tint: 8%`, `--kira-task-tint-hover: 14%` (B: 12%,
  plus `--kira-task-tint-body: 6%`).
- `apps/kira-space/frontend/src/ade/v2/AdeTaskBand.vue` (`<script setup lang="ts">`): props
  `color: string`, `variant: 'panel' | 'titlebar'`; root `relative task-tint` with inline
  `--task-color`; default slot; `ColorLine variant="under"` at the bottom (`panel` reserves `pb-0.75`
  as `AdePanelFrame` does today; `titlebar` overlays the bar's last 3px as today). `data-testid`
  `ade-task-band`, colour line keeps `ade-task-line` / `ade-review-task-colour` test ids.
- Users: `AdePanelFrame` head (task, branch, archived panels), `AdeReviewHeader`,
  `AdeTaskFilesWindow`. Plan card and span rows use `task-tint-vars` + taskGrid constants.
